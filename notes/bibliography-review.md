preprint and the reproduction repository. Background citations distinguish
multiplication-based ring graphs from nonzero-intersection adjacency, and

tensor context. They are not citations for the new nonintegrality theorems.

## Google Scholar scope and ambiguous suggestions

Google Scholar returned usable results for Beck, the Anderson–Livingston
query, Behboodi–Rakeei II and Aalipour et al. The Part I query returned
Part II, so Part I was verified from the separate Crossref entry. Subsequent
Scholar queries triggered a JavaScript/captcha interstitial and were not
treated as successful verifications. The remaining adopted works use the
publisher records linked above; no captcha was bypassed and no all-Scholar
verification is claimed.

The bare suggestions Godsil(1997), Cameron(1991) and
Chattopadhyay–Patra–Sahoo(2020) did not identify a sufficiently specific
relevant spectral work in the checks performed. They were not added from
memory. The Chattopadhyay search returned vertex-connectivity articles,
which do not support the Laplacian assertions in this paper. Cardoso et al.
provides the directly relevant generalized-join reference instead.
