\section{Positive identities for the largest-root completion}
\label{sec:largest-root-identities}

Put $s=a+b+c$, $M=bc+s$ and
\[
 T=(2b^2-ab+b-a)c^2-[(a-1)b^2+a^2]c-ab(a+b),\qquad P=h_C(M+1).
\]
For $a=3+m$, $b=a+3+u$, $c=a^2-a+v$, each row below gives the
ascending $m$ coefficient vector of $u^i v^j$. All rows and all
coefficients are displayed: $36$ terms for $T$, $170$ for $P$.
Every coefficient is positive. Thus the identities establish the two
signs on the unbounded real domain with $a\geq3$, rather than by extrapolating
from integer controls.

The independent Schur reconstruction uses
$L_t=(x-s)(x-t)-xabc/t$ and the polynomial identity
\[
 \det(xI-C)=L_aL_bL_c+a(x-a)L_bL_c+b(x-b)L_aL_c+c(x-c)L_aL_b.
\]
This specifies $P$ directly and agrees with the support-derived determinant.

\subsection{The upper sign for minima between two and three}

Put $a=2+m$, $b=5+m+u$, $c=b+v$, with $0\leq m\leq1$ and $u,v\geq0$,
and expand $P=\sum p_{kij}m^ku^iv^j$. Define
$B=\sum_{i,j}(p_{0ij}+\sum_{k\geq1,\,p_{kij}<0}p_{kij})u^iv^j$.
The exact identity
\[
 P-B=\sum_{k\geq1,\,p_{kij}>0}p_{kij}m^ku^iv^j
       +\sum_{k\geq1,\,p_{kij}<0}|p_{kij}|(1-m^k)u^iv^j\geq0
\]
holds on this slab. The following complete ascending $u$ vectors of $v^j$
give $B$ directly from the determinant above. All $35$ coefficients
are positive, so $P\geq B\geq259876>0$.
