\section{Endpoint surfaces and their reductions}
\label{sec:endpoint-reduction}

The positive quotient root in $(0,3)$ reduces possible integer spectra
to the two surfaces $h_C(1)=0$ and $h_C(2)=0$. Their size, divisor and
finite-certificate restrictions are collected together.

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:endpoint-reduction}
For positive ordered exponents $a\leq b\leq c$, put
$R(a)=7a-16+40/(a+2)$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $a\geq4$, then $c\geq R(a)$ gives $h_C(2)>0$, so
$h_C(2)=0$ requires $c<R(a)$. In this range, all-even triples and
permutations of $(1,3,2)$ or $(3,3,0)$ modulo four are nonintegral
when $c\geq R(a)$. For $a\geq8$, $c\geq7a-12$ suffices.
\item For $a,b\geq4$, an endpoint zero requires, respectively,
\begin{align*}
 h_C(1)=0&\ \Longrightarrow\ c\mid(a-1)(b-1)(a+b-1)(ab+a+b-1),\\
 h_C(2)=0&\ \Longrightarrow\ c\mid2(a-2)(b-2)(a+b-2)(ab+a+b-2).
\end{align*}
\item Every triple with $b\leq15$ is nonintegral. Every permutation
of $(2,2,2)$ or $(1,2,2)$ modulo three is also nonintegral.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The full endpoint polynomial and positivity proof are in
Appendix~\ref{sec:endpoint-details}. Its constant terms give the divisor
conditions. For the remaining ordered pairs $4\leq a<b\leq15$, the
complete certificate checks all $132$ endpoint cubics over $66$ pairs:
$8\,527$ positive constant-term divisors, including every one of the
$7\,585$ divisors above $b$, give no endpoint zero. Repeated exponents
and minima at most three were already settled. Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-low-root}
then proves the middle-exponent claim. The two modulo-three classes have
both endpoint values nonzero, as shown by the reductions in Appendix C.
\end{proof}

Finitely many endpoint-value congruences alone do not eliminate every
candidate, even with the listed gcd, valuation and geometric restrictions:
Appendix~\ref{sec:endpoint-details} gives explicit CRT constructions
below both maximum bounds and outside the sufficient inertia exclusion.
This limits these endpoint tests; another-root obstruction or further
arithmetic information can still exclude the candidates. The fully distinct
endpoint surfaces remain open.
