\section{Complete endpoint-two positive identities and finite base}
\label{sec:endpoint-two-identities}

This appendix supplies all coefficients used in
Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoint-two-rectangle} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint-two-completion}. A vector
$[v_0,\ldots,v_k]$ represents $\sum_{\ell=0}^k v_\ell m^\ell$.
The identities are coefficient identities for the generic quotient,
rather than values sampled at exponent triples.
