\begin{abstract}
We study Laplacian integrality for the graph of nonzero proper ideals of
$\mathbb Z_n$, where distinct ideals are adjacent when their intersection
is nonzero. For squarefree composite $n$, the graph is Laplacian integral
exactly when $n$ has two prime factors. For three distinct primes we prove
nonintegrality for exponent vectors $(1,1,k)$, $k\geq1$, and
$(a,a,b)$ whenever $a\geq2$ and $b>(a-3)(2a-3)/(2a+3)$.
The repeated-exponent problem admits an exact finite divisor reduction for
each fixed $a$; we settle every $b\geq1$ when $a=2,3,4,5,6$.
The proofs use small invariant subspaces, a cubic obstruction on a boundary
family, and arithmetic constraints on a quadratic discriminant.
\end{abstract}
